Robinhood stopped paying your gas.
Your coin can.

Creators fill a gas tank for their Pons token. Every buy through FUEL gets its gas paid back from that tank, in the same transaction. Any wallet works.

Reading tanks…

A refund never exceeds the trade fee the buy paid, so wash-buying your tank dry costs the attacker more than it takes. You can pause or close the tank and take the rest back at any time.

1 · FillAnyone opens or refuels a tank for any Pons token still on its curve. Tanks opened by the token's creator get a badge.
2 · BuyBuy through FUEL. The station buys on the official Pons curve and sends the tokens straight to you.
3 · RefundYour gas (L2 + L1 data fee) comes back from the tank in the same transaction, up to the tank's caps.
