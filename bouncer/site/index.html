    Check it at the door

    Paste a token. BOUNCER reads the chain itself and answers the five questions you would ask before buying — is this the right token, can they take it from you, can you sell it right now, what would you get out, who else is inside. No key, no wallet, nothing signed.

    Demo data You are looking at an invented example chain. Switch to Live to check a real token.
    Live mode reads the chain straight from your browser. If the public RPC refuses browser requests ("Failed to fetch"), deploy the read-only proxy from the repo's proxy/ folder (3 minutes, free) and paste its URL above, or use the Chrome extension, which can call any RPC.
    No key · no signer · no transaction path.
